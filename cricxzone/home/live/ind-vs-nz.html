Venue & Pitch Report

Hagley Oval traditionally offers assistance to seamers, particularly with the new ball. The surface was reported to have an even grass covering of around 13–14 mm, with pace and bounce expected early in the match.

Cool Christchurch conditions and early moisture can help swing and seam movement. Batting conditions may become more manageable once the ball softens and the pitch settles.

Conditions can change through the five days; this is a pre-match ground and curator assessment, not a guarantee.

Match Preview & Context

The second and final Test carries series and World Test Championship importance. After the opening Test finished in a draw, both sides entered Christchurch looking for a result. India chose to bowl after winning the toss, aiming to use the fresh surface and seam-friendly morning conditions. New Zealand's experienced top order and pace attack will look to make home conditions count.

Expert Outlook

Hagley Oval's grass and pace can reward disciplined fast bowling, while partnerships and patience remain crucial in a five-day contest. The toss decision reflected the potential for early assistance, but the match outcome depends on execution across all innings.
