Cookie and stream limitations

A CRICZONE page cannot create or read cookies for hotstar.com or its CDN domain. Cookies are scoped by domain; HttpOnly cookies are not readable by JavaScript. Cross-origin cookies are sent only if the browser already has eligible cookies and the provider permits credentialed CORS.

This player does not extract, copy, forge, or bypass provider authentication, DRM, or access restrictions. If the stream is protected or the CDN rejects cross-origin playback, use the provider's authorized playback flow.
